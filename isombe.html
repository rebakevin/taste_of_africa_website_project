Isombe

Rwanda · East & Southern Africa

A Rwandan favourite, isombe stews pounded cassava leaves with eggplant, spinach and peanut paste until thick and savoury. Often brightened with onion and garlic, it's served alongside matoke or rice.

Ingredients

Servings 4
  • 500 g cassava leaves, pounded
  • 1 eggplant, diced
  • 0.5 cup peanut paste
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 1 handful spinach
  • 2 cups water

Instructions

Four steps to silky isombe
  1. Prepare the leaves Rinse and pound cassava leaves to a fine paste (or use frozen, thawed).
  2. Sauté Soften onion and garlic in oil; add eggplant and cook 5 minutes.
  3. Simmer Add leaves and water; cover and simmer 45 minutes, stirring now and then.
  4. Finish Stir in peanut paste and spinach; cook 10 more minutes until thick and glossy.
