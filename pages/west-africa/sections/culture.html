Food culture

West Africa's food is bold, generous and built for sharing. From Senegal's coast to Nigeria's delta, meals centre on a big pot, a tomato-pepper base and plenty of heat.

Staples

Rice, yams, cassava, plantain and millet anchor the plate, while scotch bonnet, grains of selim, egusi seeds and palm oil give the region its signature flavours.

Cooking techniques

Slow simmering in heavy pots, open-fire grilling (think suya), pounding fufu in a mortar, and smoking fish for deep savoury flavour.
