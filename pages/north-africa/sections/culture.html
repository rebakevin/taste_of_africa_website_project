Food culture

Shaped by the Mediterranean and the Sahara, North African cooking balances sweet and savoury, fragrant and fiery, all perfumed with warm spices and preserved lemon.

Staples

Wheat in the form of couscous and bread, chickpeas, lentils and olives sit beside cumin, coriander, harissa, saffron and ras el hanout.

Cooking techniques

Slow tagine braising in conical clay pots, steaming couscous over stew, charring vegetables and preserving lemons in salt.
