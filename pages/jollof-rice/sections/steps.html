Instructions

Four steps to smoky jollof
  1. Make the base Blend tomatoes, peppers and onion; fry in oil with tomato paste until the water cooks off and the base turns deep red, about 25 minutes.
  2. Season Stir in thyme, curry powder, bay leaf and salt. Add stock and bring to a boil.
  3. Cook the rice Wash the rice, add it to the pot, cover tightly with foil and cook on the lowest heat for 30 minutes.
  4. Rest & serve Fluff, let the bottom catch a little for that smoky “bottom pot” edge, and serve with fried plantain.
