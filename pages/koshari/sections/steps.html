Instructions

Four steps to layered koshari
  1. Cook the grains Boil lentils until just tender. Cook rice and macaroni separately and drain.
  2. Fry the onions Fry sliced onions in oil until deep golden and crisp; drain on paper.
  3. Spiced tomato sauce Sauté garlic and cumin, add crushed tomatoes and a splash of vinegar; simmer 15 minutes.
  4. Assemble Layer rice, lentils, pasta and chickpeas; top with sauce and the crispy onions.
