Bunny Chow

South Africa · East & Southern Africa

Created by Durban's Indian community in the 1940s, bunny chow packs a fragrant curry into a hollowed-out loaf. Built on garam masala, turmeric, chilli and ginger, it's eaten by hand, using the bread as spoon.

Ingredients

Servings 4
  • 600 g chicken or lamb, cubed
  • 2 tbsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 2 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 2 potatoes, cubed
  • 1 can chopped tomatoes
  • 1 white loaf, quartered

Instructions

Four steps to bunny chow
  1. Bloom the spices Fry onion, ginger and garlic; add garam masala, turmeric and chilli and toast 1 minute.
  2. Brown the meat Add the meat and tomatoes; cook until the meat is sealed and the sauce thickens.
  3. Simmer Add potatoes and water; simmer 40 minutes until tender and rich.
  4. Fill the loaf Hollow out each quarter loaf, ladle in the curry and top with the bread plug.
