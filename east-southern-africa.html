East & Southern Africa

Flatbreads, braai fires and curry-spiced comfort.

Food culture

From Ethiopia's highlands to the South African braai, this region's cooking is as varied as its landscapes: fermented teff, maize porridge, leafy greens and grilled meats.

Staples

Teff, maize (ugali, pap), sorghum, cassava leaves, beans and plantain form the base, flavoured with berbere, piri-piri, curry leaf and groundnuts.

Cooking techniques

Open-fire braai and grilling, fermenting batters for injera, slow-stewing greens and spice-blending from Indian Ocean trade.
