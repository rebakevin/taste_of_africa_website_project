Injera

Ethiopia · East & Southern Africa

Ethiopia's everyday bread is made from teff, a tiny ancient grain, fermented for days until bubbly and tangy, then poured onto a clay mitad griddle. It is both plate and utensil for stews like doro wat spiced with berbere.

Ingredients

Servings 4
  • 2 cups teff flour
  • 3 cups water, plus more
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 0.25 cup starter from a previous batch

Instructions

Four steps to tangy injera
  1. Mix the batter Whisk teff flour with water (and starter, if using) into a smooth, pourable batter.
  2. Ferment Cover loosely and rest at room temperature for 2–3 days until bubbly and sour.
  3. Thin & salt Stir in salt and enough water to reach thin crêpe-batter consistency.
  4. Cook Pour in a spiral onto a hot non-stick pan, cover and cook until eyes form on the surface. Do not flip.
